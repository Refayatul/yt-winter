Failure Reconstructed

Forensic engineering documentaries — short reconstructions of real failures.

Every episode takes one engineering failure apart: what broke, why it broke, and what changed afterwards. Footage is real archival film (public domain) or licensed stock. Narration uses a synthetic voice, which is disclosed on every video.

The uploader tool

This site also hosts the policies for Failure Reconstructed uploader, a personal automation tool that publishes the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It has exactly one user: its author.
