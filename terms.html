Terms of Service — Failure Reconstructed uploader

Last updated: 26 September 2026

What this application is

"Failure Reconstructed uploader" is a personal, non-commercial automation tool used by a single creator to upload their own short documentary videos to their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

Source code: https://github.com/eyazan/youtube-otomasyon

What it does

  • Uploads video files that the creator produced to the creator's own TikTok inbox, using the TikTok Content Posting API with the video.upload scope.
  • The uploaded video appears as a draft in the creator's TikTok app. Nothing is published automatically; the creator reviews and posts each video manually.
  • It does not read, collect or modify any other TikTok content, and it does not interact with any account other than the creator's own.

Content

All videos uploaded through this application are produced by the creator from public domain archival film or licensed stock footage, with attribution recorded in the repository. Narration uses a synthetic (text-to-speech) voice, which is disclosed in the video description on every platform.

Acceptable use

Because this tool has exactly one user — its author — there is no third-party access to grant or revoke. The author agrees to use it only in accordance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
