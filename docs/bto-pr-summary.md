## Why
BTO research was rejecting ordinary-object questions before evidence evaluation because Wikipedia article titles had to closely match the object name.

## What
Adds strict-first semantic fallback discovery, authority-host ranking utilities, regression/safety tests and live smoke cases. Search results are discovery metadata only. Existing primary-source, reachability, verbatim-quote, narration support and Builder quality gates remain unchanged.

## Validation
PR CI runs offline regressions and live Wikipedia smoke discovery for street numbers, coffee shops and hash-symbol cases.
