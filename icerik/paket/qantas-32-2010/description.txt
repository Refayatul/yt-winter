Qantas 32 suffered an uncontained engine failure. The cause: misaligned counterbore thinning an oil feed stub pipe, leading to fatigue, oil fire and turbine disc failure.

Failure chain: Pipe bored off-centre → Wall too thin → Fatigue crack leaks oil → Oil fire → Turbine disc bursts
