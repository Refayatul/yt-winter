Columbia broke apart during re-entry. The cause: foam strike breaching a reinforced carbon-carbon panel on the wing's leading edge.

Failure chain: Foam breaks off external tank → Strikes left wing leading edge → Hole in heat shield panel → Hot gas enters wing on re-entry → Orbiter breaks apart
