Hyatt Regency collapsed onto a crowded hotel lobby. The cause: box-beam connection pull-through from a doubled hanger-rod load.

Failure chain: One rod becomes two → Load on upper box beam doubles → Nut pulls through box beam → Upper walkway falls onto lower → Both hit the crowded lobby
