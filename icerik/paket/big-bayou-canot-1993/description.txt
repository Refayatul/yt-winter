Big Bayou Canot derailed a passenger train into a bayou. The cause: barge impact displacing a span and kinking the track without breaking the signal circuit.

Failure chain: Towboat lost in fog → Barge hits the bridge → Track kinked, rails not broken → Signals stay green → Train derails into Bayou
