Saudia 163 landed after a cargo fire, but no one escaped. The cause: cabin fire and smoke with evacuation never started after landing.

Failure chain: Smoke warning in cargo hold → Slow return to airport → Lands and taxis → Doors not opened → Fire and smoke fill cabin
