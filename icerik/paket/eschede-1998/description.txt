Eschede derailed and crashed into a bridge. The cause: fatigue fracture of a rubber-cushioned wheel's outer tyre, which then struck the points.

Failure chain: Rubber-cushioned wheel design → Tyre wears thin → Fatigue crack → Tyre breaks, hits points → Carriages slam into bridge
