Beirut was destroyed by a massive explosion. The cause: fire heating a large, long-stored stockpile of ammonium nitrate until it detonated.

Failure chain: Seized cargo stored for years → Warnings filed and ignored → Fire breaks out → Ammonium nitrate detonates → Port and city devastated
