USS Thresher was lost during a deep-diving test. The cause: probable flooding from a piping joint, reactor shutdown, and ice blocking the emergency ballast blow.

Failure chain: Likely pipe joint leak → Reactor shuts down → Emergency blow attempted → Ice blocks air strainers → Sub sinks past crush depth
