Three Mile Island partially melted down. The cause: loss of coolant through a stuck-open pilot-operated relief valve, misread by operators.

Failure chain: Feedwater pumps trip → Relief valve opens and sticks → Indicator shows command, not position → Operators cut emergency cooling → Core partially melts
