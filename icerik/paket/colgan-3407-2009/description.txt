Colgan 3407 stalled and crashed into a house near Buffalo. The cause: inappropriate response to the stick shaker, deepening an aerodynamic stall.

Failure chain: Speed decays on approach → Stick shaker activates → Captain pulls back → Stall deepens → Aircraft crashes
