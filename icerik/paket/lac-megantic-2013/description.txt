Lac-Mégantic rolled into town, derailed and exploded. The cause: air brakes releasing after the locomotive was shut down, with insufficient handbrakes to hold the train.

Failure chain: Train parked on a grade → Too few handbrakes → Locomotive fire, engine shut down → Air brakes leak off → Runaway derails in town
