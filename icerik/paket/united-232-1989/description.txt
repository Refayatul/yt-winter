United 232 lost all flight controls. The cause: fatigue crack from a manufacturing defect shattering the tail engine's fan disk and severing all three hydraulic systems.

Failure chain: Defect in titanium forging → Fatigue crack grows → Fan disk shatters → Debris cuts all three hydraulics → Crash landing at sioux city
