SL-1 exploded during maintenance. The cause: prompt criticality from manual withdrawal of the central control rod, causing a steam explosion.

Failure chain: Maintenance requires lifting a rod → Rod pulled too far → Prompt criticality → Steam explosion → Vessel jumps upward
