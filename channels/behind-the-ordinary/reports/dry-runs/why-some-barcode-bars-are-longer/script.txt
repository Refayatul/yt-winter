Some barcode bars are longer, and that's deliberate. They hold no number at all. On a UPC barcode, the start, middle and end guard bars extend lower. They encode no digit; they split the code into two halves. IBM's George Laurer was assigned to design a supermarket scanner label. His team defined guard bars so a scanner could detect the label. They even told it which half it was reading. So the longest bars are signposts, not data.
