That strange Bluetooth symbol hides two letters. Two runes, merged into one symbol. The name honours King Harald Bluetooth, who united Denmark and Norway. At an industry meeting, Intel's Jim Kardach suggested it as a temporary code name. Harald united Scandinavia. This radio link would unite PCs and phones. It was only a placeholder. RadioWire or PAN was meant to replace it. PAN was already everywhere online, and RadioWire's trademark check ran out of time. So the logo merges Harald's initials in runes: H and B.
