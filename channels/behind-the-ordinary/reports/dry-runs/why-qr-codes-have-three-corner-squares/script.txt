Those three QR squares aren't decoration. They tell the scanner where to look. Denso Wave released the QR Code in 1994, and QR means quick response. The corner marks are position detection patterns. Finding them made high-speed reading possible. Why squares? The team said that pattern was least likely to appear on business forms. They surveyed flyers, magazines and cardboard boxes for the rarest black-and-white ratio. That rare ratio lets a scanner orient the code from any angle.
