Every shot has an authored category, background, layout and motion, so the reusable visual-diversity diagnostics ran **before any frame exists**. The results are in `storyboard-visual-diversity.md`. Assets are original SVG motion graphics built on `brand-lib.js` (`PD.environment`, `PD.evidenceCard`, `PD.sourceCompact`). There is no stock, no footage, no logos and no facsimiles. Every sourced figure carries a compact on-screen citation.

Two new reusable settings are proposed for `PD.environment`: **airport gate** and **kitchen table**. They join the existing desk, café, checkout and restaurant settings.
